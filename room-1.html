Alyssa

KONG
PLAY.

A playful study of color, balance, and scale.

About the work01—01

Small forms,
big personalities.

Kong Play brings together glossy, brightly colored spheres placed across a series of soft, irregular platforms. The objects vary in size and color, giving each group its own rhythm while keeping the composition connected as a whole.

The work feels playful without being chaotic. The raised platforms create a sense of depth, while the repeated round forms invite the eye to move from one cluster to another. It is a simple visual language built around curiosity, arrangement, and the pleasure of looking.

Artwork: Kong Play, 2025. Emilie Louise Gossiaux.
