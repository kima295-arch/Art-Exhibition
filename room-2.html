SOME.

A geometric composition built from contrast and balance.

About the work02—02

Geometry with
a point of view.

In Some, Ulrike Müller uses a small vocabulary of geometric shapes to create a composition that feels both structured and unexpected. A large dark-green form anchors the image, while a pale-blue curve, a soft pink triangle, and a light grey field interrupt its weight.

The shapes meet at sharp edges and broad curves, creating a quiet tension between order and movement. The limited palette keeps the composition focused, allowing proportion, negative space, and the relationship between each shape to do the work.

Artwork: Some, 2017. Ulrike Müller.
